Our Mission

To empower underprivileged students by providing educational support, essential resources, and meaningful community engagement, fostering a culture of continuous learning, resilience, and personal growth.

Learn More

Our Values

Education for Empowerment: Providing quality learning opportunities to help children build skills and create brighter futures.

Inclusivity & Equality: Ensuring that every child, regardless of background, has an equal opportunity to succeed.

Community & Collaboration: Fostering teamwork and partnerships to create sustainable educational impact.

Our Impact

TWEENS has significantly impacted underprivileged students through education and community support. Up to 200 students benefit from tutoring, study spaces, Wi-Fi, and learning materials.

Our Activities

Community

Big to young sister mentorship

Older, more experienced students mentoring younger girls, offering academic guidance, emotional support, and motivation to help them succeed in their studies and personal growth.

Girls book club

A safe and engaging space where girls come together to read, discuss books, and develop a love for learning while fostering discussions on empowerment and leadership.

Parent Teacher Association

Parents, teachers, and mentors collaborate to discuss students' academic progress, challenges, and well-being to create a nurturing learning environment.

A level study program

Academic support, resources, and mentorship for students preparing for A-Level exams, including structured study sessions and guidance on scholarships.

Education awareness campaigns

Advocating for the importance of education through motivational talks, workshops, and outreach that inspire learners, parents, and stakeholders.

Student seminars and workshops

Interactive learning experiences where students gain academic, career, and personal development skills through expert talks and mentorship.

O-Level tutoring and homework support

Personalized academic assistance to improve exam performance, build confidence, and strengthen academic skills.

Extracurricular offerings

Opportunities to explore activities beyond academics such as sports, arts, leadership programs, and social clubs.

Clean up campaigns

Promoting environmental awareness and responsibility within the community through cleanup activities and waste management education.
